## Priority signals for the first roadmap

The strongest recurrence signals should initially be used to build study blocks around:

1. Family Medicine principles + screening
2. Common paediatrics/neonatology
3. Obstetrics/gynaecology + contraception
4. Dermatology
5. Infectious/tropical medicine relevant to primary care
6. Psychiatry
7. Diabetes/CVD/hypertension
8. Respiratory disease + emergencies
9. Gastroenterology
10. Musculoskeletal medicine
11. ENT/ophthalmology
12. Urology/sexual health

**Caution:** This ordering is a planning signal from the uploaded corpus, not a ranking of official exam importance. It should be revised when an official curriculum/blueprint and additional papers are available.
